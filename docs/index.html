04 / About

Engineering for the moment something goes wrong.

I describe my strongest skill as adaptability. I approach engineering and security as professional puzzle solving: understand the objective, use the right tools, and stay flexible enough to find a practical path forward.

That “fixer” mindset now combines a software engineering foundation with hands-on work in physical security, RFID, community, and risk management.

The goal is not to promise perfect systems. It is to understand failure modes, reduce exposure, and make recovery part of the design.
